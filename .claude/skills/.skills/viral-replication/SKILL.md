# Gold Smith Viral Replication

Replicate **packaging**, not identity.

Keep what works structurally: hook tension, body rhythm, visual hierarchy, CTA mechanic. Replace substance with Gold Smith finance insight, risk discipline, and brand voice.

## Required Context

Read:

1. `CLAUDE.md`
2. `Gold-Smith-fb.md`
3. `context/voice-analysis.md`
4. `.claude/rules/safety.md`
5. `.claude/rules/design.md`

Optional:

- `reference/adam-robinson-writing-style.md` for packaging mechanics only.
- `reference/adam-robinson-top-posts.md` for examples of hook/body/CTA structure only.
- `reference/carousel-ref/carousel-style-analysis.md` for carousel structure.

## What To Keep

- Hook type: contradiction, hard truth, mistake, list, before/after, tension.
- Body structure: setup -> proof/reasoning -> lesson -> CTA.
- Visual information architecture.
- CTA mechanic if it is soft and safe.

## What To Replace

- Voice: always Gold Smith, not Adam Robinson or any creator being studied.
- Topic: finance, market psychology, capital discipline, personal finance, founder cash flow, fintech behavior.
- Claims: only verified or framed as opinion/lesson.
- CTA: no aggressive selling, no promise, no FOMO.
- Visual style: Gold Smith palette and `.claude/rules/design.md`.

## Process

1. Capture the reference:
   - hook
   - body sections
   - CTA
   - visual layout
   - why it works
2. Choose Gold Smith angle:
   - persona
   - pillar
   - market/audience problem
   - safety layer
3. Draft with the same packaging logic.
4. Run safety review.
5. Run voice edit.
6. Add visual/repurpose direction.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not copy personal details, numbers, achievements, or claims from the reference.
- Do not make Gold Smith sound like a SaaS founder unless the brief is founder/fintech.
- Do not turn market content into a signal service.
