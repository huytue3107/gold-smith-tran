## Viec Can Lam

1. Liet ke bai/post/draft/output lien quan trong ky review (sync voi `outputs/content-ledger.md`).
2. Phan loai theo persona, pillar, platform, format.
3. Kiem tra bai nao thieu metadata/hook/CTA/visual/safety.
4. Neu co analytics that, tong hop pattern.
5. Neu chua co analytics, ghi `Data quality: Qualitative only`.
6. Voi bai underperform (engagement thap, saves thap, comment lac), goi skill `content-debugging` de phan tich systematic theo 7 buoc thay vi doi angle ngau nhien.
7. De xuat 3-5 next actions cho 7 ngay toi.
8. Update `outputs/content-ledger.md` voi metrics 24h/7d va Lesson neu co.
